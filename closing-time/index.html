Restore the Evening Collection

Use the Apple Account that made the purchase, then choose Restore purchases in Settings or on the collection page. If the collection is still locked, check your connection and follow any sign-in prompt from Apple. The one-time purchase unlocks Rainy Chapter and Afterglow Flowers, including their rituals, music, ambience and share cards. The complete tea shop and local journal are free.

Payments and refunds

Apple handles payment. For a purchase problem or refund request, visit Apple purchase support. Eligibility depends on Apple and applicable consumer rights.

Sound is quiet

Open the headphones button or Settings. Action sounds, music and ambience have separate controls. Check the phone volume and Silent Mode; music and ambience start off. Optional audio plays only while the app is active and stops when you leave or background it.

Save, delete or export your words

Your journal is local. In Journal, edit or delete an entry, or export a file. In Settings, discard an unfinished note or erase the journal, draft and shop name. Export anything you want to keep before erasing. Sharing a closing card does not include your notes or custom shop name.

Moving to another phone

Restore your purchase with the Apple Account you used to buy it. Moving your journal and drafts depends on iOS device migration or a device backup. Export important entries before switching phones. You can read exported entries outside the app.

恢复小店合集

使用购买时的 Apple 账户，在设置或合集页选择「恢复购买」。如果仍未解锁，请检查网络，并按 Apple 的提示登录。一次购买即可解锁雨中书屋和余晖花铺，包括各自的关店仪式、音乐、环境声及分享卡。完整茶铺和本机手记免费使用。

付款与退款

付款由 Apple 处理。遇到购买问题或需要申请退款，可前往Apple 购买支持。退款资格以 Apple 规则及适用消费者权利为准。

听不到声音

打开耳机按钮或设置，分别检查动作音效、音乐、环境声、音量及手机静音模式。音乐与环境声默认关闭；可选声音只在App前台播放，离开或进入后台即停止。

手记与换机

在手记里可编辑、删除或导出记录。在设置里可丢弃未完成的便笺，或清除手记、草稿和店名。清除前，请先导出要保留的内容。分享卡只显示小店场景和日期。

换机后可以用购买时的 Apple 账户恢复购买。手记和草稿的迁移取决于 iOS 的设备迁移或备份。换机前请导出重要内容，导出的文字可在 App 外阅读。
