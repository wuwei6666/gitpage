Closing Time privacy policy

This policy covers the Closing Time app and its support pages. The developer is wuwei tang. For privacy questions or requests, email 1529891141@qq.com.

Effective November 16, 2026.

Inside the app

Content stays on your device

Notes, selected feelings, unfinished drafts, shop personalization and entry dates/time-zone context are stored locally. Closing Time does not upload your journal or use a server to interpret it. The app has no account system, advertising, tracking SDK or analytics SDK. Language selection does not use GPS.

You choose what to share

You can edit/delete entries, export the journal, discard a draft or erase personal content in Settings. An export contains private words; you choose its destination. Closing cards contain the shop scene and date, without your notes or custom shop name. Your chosen recipient or sharing service handles the file under its own practices.

Backups and migration

Device backups or device migration may include local app data. Closing Time does not operate a separate cloud-backup or sync service. iOS and your backup settings control those copies. Deleting content in the app does not independently erase older system backups.

Purchases

Apple processes payments. StoreKit checks your purchase on your device to unlock or restore the collection. The developer does not receive your card details. Apple handles its services under its own privacy policy.

Apple sends signed purchase and refund notifications to a service hosted by Railway in California, United States. TestFlight transactions are processed in a separate test environment. The service verifies each notification and records the product, status, event identifiers and timestamps, a notification digest, and transaction identifiers transformed with a keyed hash. These transaction references can still be matched to the same purchase. We use these records to reconcile purchases and refunds, prevent duplicate entries and investigate purchase problems. Original transaction identifiers and signed messages are processed for verification and excluded from stored records. The purchase records exclude your email address, journal and feelings.

Sound and reminders

Music and ambience are bundled files played locally, without microphone access or an audio-generation service. Optional reminders are scheduled locally after notification permission. You can turn these features off.

If you contact support

Email is optional. We receive your email address, any sender name included in the message, and the message and files you choose to send. We use these details to answer and manage your request. Please leave out private notes, passwords and payment-card details. Your mail provider and Tencent QQ Mail process these messages. Processing may occur outside your country.

Retention and your requests

We keep purchase records while they are needed to reconcile purchases, refunds and disputes for the collection. We keep support correspondence while we handle your request and any related follow-up. We delete records when these purposes end, subject to applicable legal recordkeeping requirements. Encrypted copies made for service recovery expire as backups rotate. Your journal and drafts are managed separately on your device.

Email 1529891141@qq.com to request access, correction or deletion of information we hold. We may ask for the minimum details needed to verify your request and locate the relevant record. Contact Apple purchase support about Apple payment records or refund decisions.

Reading these pages

These static pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security under its Privacy Statement. The pages have no analytics scripts, ads or forms. When you follow an external link, the destination service handles your visit under its own practices.

Changes

If a future feature changes these practices, we will update this policy before the changed handling applies and obtain any required permission. Contact the developer with questions.

《打烊了》隐私政策

本机保存

便笺、主动选择的心情、未完成草稿、店名与小物、记录日期及所在时区信息保存在设备上。App不上传日记或使用服务端分析私人文字；没有账号、广告、追踪或统计SDK。语言选择不会调用GPS。

由你决定分享什么

可以编辑、删除记录、导出手记、丢弃草稿或在设置清除个人内容。导出含私人文字，目的地由你选择。分享卡仅含小店场景与日期，不含便笺或自定义店名；接收方与分享服务按各自规则处理文件。

备份与换机

系统备份和设备迁移可能包含本机数据。App没有独立云备份或同步服务，副本由iOS及你的备份设置管理。App内删除不会单独清除旧的系统备份。

购买

Apple 处理付款。StoreKit 在设备上核对购买权益，用于解锁或恢复合集。开发者不接收银行卡资料。Apple 服务适用其自身隐私政策。

Apple 将带签名的购买及退款通知发送给托管在美国加利福尼亚州 Railway 的服务。TestFlight 测试交易由独立环境处理。服务核验通知后，记录商品、状态、通知标识、时间、通知摘要，以及经过带密钥哈希处理的交易标识。处理后的交易标识仍可关联到同一笔购买。我们用这些记录核对购买和退款、防止重复入账，并排查购买问题。原始交易标识和签名通知仅用于核验，购买记录不保存这些原始内容，也不保存你的邮箱、手记或心情。

声音与提醒

音乐和环境声是随 App 提供的本地文件，不使用麦克风或声音生成服务。提醒经通知授权后在本机安排，可以关闭。

主动联系支持时

你可以选择发邮件。我们会收到你的邮箱、随邮件显示的发件人名称，以及你主动发送的消息和附件，用于回复和处理请求。请避免发送私人便笺、密码或银行卡资料。邮件由你选择的邮件服务和腾讯 QQ 邮箱处理，处理地点可能在你所在国家或地区之外。

保留与隐私请求

在需要核对合集购买、退款或处理争议期间，我们保留购买记录。处理请求及相关后续事项期间，我们保留客服邮件。上述用途结束后，我们删除相关记录，适用法律要求保留的记录除外。用于服务恢复的加密副本随备份轮换到期。你的手记和草稿在设备上单独管理。

如要查阅、更正或删除我们持有的信息，请发邮件至 1529891141@qq.com。我们可能请求验证身份和定位记录所需的最少信息。有关 Apple 付款记录或退款结果，请联系 Apple 购买支持。

浏览本网页时

这些静态页面由 GitHub Pages 托管。GitHub 为安全目的记录访客 IP 地址，详见其隐私声明。页面没有统计脚本、广告或表单。打开外部链接后，由目标服务按其自身规则处理访问。

后续变更

若新功能改变处理方式，会在变更生效前更新政策并取得必要授权。有问题可联系开发者。
